## Measurements

The native scaling fixture creates three independent worlds for each of 30 construction cases. The six occupied counts are 128, 256, 512, 1,024, 2,048 and 4,096. Solid construction alternates Stone and Brick. The sparse lattice places cells two units apart along each local axis. The hollow structure is a 17-cell-wide perimeter extruded upwards. The irregular deck offsets successive bands. Mixed construction replaces every 64th cube with an oriented slab, wedge or stair. All cases use unit cells through 2,048 and half-unit cells at 4,096. A disconnected rigid lattice remains one deliberately authored body. [Fixture and generators](../../tests/SpatialConstructionCompoundScale.inl)

The fixture measures field mutation, static preparation, native dynamic activation, synchronous velocity edits plus publication, 30 free-motion steps with a property observer, rotated world rays, live removal and reinsertion, and 120 full fixed physics steps including actual floor contact. The observer reads each published pose and revision. Broadphase pair, narrowphase collision and solver values come from the native backend profile. Each timed query/step group reports nearest-rank p50, p95, p99 and maximum with its sample count. Three cold activations are three observations per case. Actual child count comes from the native collision receipt. Body identity and the entire inertia tensor are checked after reconstructing the edited shape. Independent unit-solid centroid and covariance integrals cover boxes, slabs, the triangular prism and the two-step solid.

`Box3DStaticBytesOverEmpty` and `Box3DDynamicBytesOverEmpty` are changes in the Box3D allocator's live-byte counter within one process and include observed native world allocations. Each world is destroyed and checked against its prior counter. `CanonicalFieldBytes` is the encoded payload length. `FieldSnapshotBudgetChargeBytes` is the storage subsystem's conservative pin charge. Application allocator overhead, compiler temporaries and driver allocations require their own measurement. The GNS fixture starts a real Server and Client over private loopback, advances the authoritative physics body every pump, checks multiple distinct installed transforms, queries the rotated replica, removes a moving cell and observes remote retirement.
